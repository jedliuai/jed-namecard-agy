Add your WeChat QR code

  1. In WeChat, open Me, tap your name, then My QR Code, and save the image.
  2. In the card editor, choose WeChat and upload that image. It goes on the back of your card.
  3. Add your WeChat ID too if you like, so people can search for you.

Questions

Why not just share my WeChat QR code?

A bare QR code says nothing about you. Your card shows it next to your name, title, company and other contacts, and people who don't use WeChat can still reach you.

Do people need WeChat to see my card?

No. Your card opens in any browser. They only need WeChat to scan your QR code and add you.

Can my card show both WeChat and WhatsApp?

Each card shows one messenger. Pick the one your clients use; you can switch any time.

Can I write my card in Chinese?

Yes. Any field can be in Chinese, English or both.
