Your business card, in 3D, in your pocket

Super Namecard makes digital business cards that tilt, flip and turn sideways like a real card. Share a link or a QR code, and people save your contact or message you on WhatsApp or WeChat in one tap. No app to install, for you or for them.

How it works

  1. Pick a style and type in your name, title, company, phone and email. The card updates as you type.
  2. Add your photo and choose WhatsApp or WeChat. The QR code on the back is made from your number, or upload your own.
  3. Share your card link, show the QR code in person, or post a video of your card on social media.

  • One-tap contact

    Visitors save your contact straight to their phone, or open a chat with you.

  • WhatsApp or WeChat

    Each card carries the QR code of the messenger your clients use.

  • Your own link

    Your card lives at yourname.1name.cards, with a proper preview when you send it.

  • Video for social

    Export an HD video with music, a GIF and still images of your card.

  • Edit any time

    Change a phone number or job title and your shared link shows it at once.

Questions

Do the people I share my card with need an app?

No. Your card opens in any phone or computer browser. They can save your contact, call, email or message you from there.

WhatsApp or WeChat?

Each card shows one, so you choose the one your clients use. For WhatsApp the QR code is made from your number. For WeChat, upload the QR code from your WeChat profile.

Can I use my own photo?

Yes. Upload any photo and crop it in the frame. Styles that show a portrait will use it.

What is in an export?

An HD video of your card turning and flipping, with the style's music, plus a GIF and JPG images of the front, back and landscape view. They are made for Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp Status.

Who can see my card?

Only people you give your link or QR code to. Card pages are kept out of search engines, and you can change your card at any time.
