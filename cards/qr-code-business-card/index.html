Questions

Do people need a QR scanner app?

No. The camera app on iPhone and Android phones reads QR codes.

What does my QR code open?

With WhatsApp, a chat with you. With WeChat, your WeChat profile so they can add you. If you upload your own code, wherever that code points.

Can I change the QR code later?

Yes. Change your number or upload a new image and your card updates.

Is a QR code business card free?

Designing your card, QR code included, is free. Your live card link and video exports come with Pro ($9.90 a year) or a share pack ($2.99).
