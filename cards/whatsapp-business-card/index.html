How the WhatsApp QR code works

Type your WhatsApp number, with its country code, and the back of your card gets a QR code that opens a chat with you. Any phone camera can scan it. The person doesn't need your number saved first.

People who open your card link instead of scanning get a WhatsApp button that does the same thing, plus Save contact to add you to their phone.

It works the same for WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business numbers. If you'd rather use a QR code you already have, upload the image and it goes on the card instead.

Yes. The QR code carries your number, so it works with WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business alike.
