Which style for real estate

Noir suits luxury listings and premium brokerages: black metal, gold type, a black-card back, and room for your photo so buyers remember your face. Swiss suits modern brands: a clean grid, a bold red disc and big type that reads well on a small screen.

Both carry your WhatsApp or WeChat QR code and a Save contact button, and you can switch between them any time without retyping.

What to put on your card

  • Tagline: Licensed agent, or your licence number
  • Job title: your area or speciality, such as Downtown condos · First-time buyers
  • Company: your brokerage
  • Phone and WhatsApp: the number buyers can text
  • Photo: in Noir, it sits beside your name

Your card updates as you type, and you can change any of it later.
