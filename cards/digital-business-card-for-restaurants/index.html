Why Pop suits food and retail

When the card opens, the letters of your name drop in like stickers and stack up with real physics. A greeting bubble sits beside them, and the back is a comic panel with your QR code and a CALL ME! burst. Your photo or logo goes in the round badge.

What to put on your card

  • Name: your brand, such as FATTY
  • Greeting: a line in a speech bubble, such as Hungry? Burgers on me!
  • Tagline: who you are, such as Burger boss
  • Company: your shop and where, such as Fatty's Burgers · 3 stores
  • Phone and WhatsApp: for bookings and catering

Your card updates as you type, and you can change any of it later.

Questions

Can customers order through the card?

The card opens a WhatsApp chat or adds you on WeChat; orders and bookings happen there. It isn't an ordering system.

Can I use my brand name instead of my own?

Yes. The name line can be your brand, like FATTY on the sample card.

Can I put my logo on it?

Upload your logo as the photo and it goes in the round badge.
