Why Noir suits a law practice

Noir looks like the cards the best firms print on heavy stock: a brushed black face, your name in gold serif type and the firm's name in spaced capitals. As the card opens, its gold lines catch the light as if freshly engraved, and as the client tilts their phone the light moves across the metal.

The back carries a magnetic stripe, a signature panel with your name and your QR code, so it reads like a premium charge card rather than a web page. Add your photo and it sits on the front beside your name.

What to put on your card

  • Tagline: your position, such as Managing Partner
  • Job title: your practice area, such as Corporate Law · M&A
  • Company: the firm's name
  • Phone and email: your direct line, not the switchboard
  • WhatsApp or WeChat: for clients who would rather message than email

Your card updates as you type, and you can change any of it later.
