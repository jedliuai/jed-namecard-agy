Questions

How long is the video?

About 15 seconds: your card's entrance, a flip to the back and back again, and a turn to landscape.

Can I export my own card, not a sample?

Yes. Exports use your own card, with your name, photo and QR code.

Is there sound?

Each style has its own music. On your card page, visitors turn it on or off with the sound button; the video export includes it.

Do I need design skills?

No. Pick a style and type your details; the layout, motion and music come with the style.
