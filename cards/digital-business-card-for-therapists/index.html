Why Aurora suits wellness work

The front is frosted glass over slowly moving colour, with your photo or initials in a ring and your specialities below your name. The back puts your QR code in a glass orb with a ring of text turning around it.

What to put on your card

  • Tagline: your credential and city, such as Licensed therapist · NYC
  • Job title: your specialities, such as Emotional healing · Couples
  • Company: your practice or studio
  • Photo: clients like to see who they'll meet
  • Phone and WhatsApp: for appointments
