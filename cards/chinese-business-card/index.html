Why a bilingual card matters

In China a business card is often handed over with both hands and read with care, and a card in Chinese shows respect to the person receiving it. A digital card adds what paper can't: the WeChat QR code most Chinese contacts will use to follow up.

Ink is rice paper, a brush-written name in a vertical column, a red seal and a crane in the mist. The back shows far hills under the moon with your details in vertical lines. On an English-only card, Latin names stand upright in the column, so nothing lies on its side.

What to put on your card

  • Name: your Chinese name, such as 苏清和
  • Tagline: your English name and role, such as Su Qinghe · Tea master
  • Job title and company: in Chinese, English or both
  • WeChat QR code: from your WeChat profile
  • Phone and email

Your card updates as you type, and you can change any of it later.
