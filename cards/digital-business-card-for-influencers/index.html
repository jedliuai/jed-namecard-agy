Why Holo suits creators

Holo is a card printed on holographic foil: soft rainbow colour across the face, your photo or monogram in a tile, a badge for your follower count, and small tags for what you do. The back runs your name as a marquee with your QR code on a foil-rimmed sticker.

What to put on your card

  • Badge: your follower count, such as 286K followers
  • Tagline: your niche and city, such as Lifestyle creator · LA
  • Job title: what you offer, shown as tags: Content · Brand deals · UGC
  • Company: your studio or management
  • Email: the address for brand deals

Your card updates as you type, and you can change any of it later.
