Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Super Namecard (1name.cards) collects and how it is used. The service is operated by Yongjun Liu ("we"), who is responsible for your data. Contact: support@1name.cards.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to create your account and send you sign-in codes.
  • What you put on your card: name, tagline, job title, company, phone, WhatsApp number or WeChat ID, email, card link name, your photo and your messenger QR code.
  • Purchases: what you bought, when, and its status (for example active, cancelled or refunded), plus the customer and subscription IDs our payment provider gives us. We never see or store your card number; Creem handles payment details.
  • Exports you order, and the files made for you.
  • Technical data: IP address and browser details in our server logs, used for security and to stop abuse. We keep them for a short time.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell your data. Your browser keeps your sign-in session and an unsaved card draft in its local storage on your own device.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service: show your card, keep it in your account and make your exports (performing our contract with you).
  • To handle payments and what you are entitled to (contract, and legal obligations for records).
  • To keep the service secure and stop spam and abuse (our legitimate interest).

Who sees it

With Pro, your card, including the contact details you put on it, is shown to anyone who has your card link or QR code. Card pages are marked so search engines do not list them.

We use these providers to run the service, and they process data only on our instructions or as independent controllers where stated:

  • Oracle Cloud: hosting of our servers and database.
  • Cloudflare: network delivery, security and the human check on sign-in (Turnstile).
  • Resend: sending sign-in code emails.
  • Creem: payments, as Merchant of Record. Creem processes your payment data under its own privacy policy.

Some of these providers process data outside your country, including in the United States, with safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Accounts that ask for a code but never sign in are deleted after 2 days.
  • Your account and card are kept until you ask us to delete them.
  • Export files are deleted 30 days after they are made.
  • Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Database backups are kept for a limited time and then overwritten.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by writing to support@1name.cards. You can change your card yourself at any time. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

The service is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above. If a change matters, we will also tell you by email.
