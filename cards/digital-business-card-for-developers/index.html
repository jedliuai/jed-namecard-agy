Why Terminal suits developers

The front reads like a shell session: $ whoami and your name typed key by key, then your role and company as command output, then your phone and email. The back draws your QR code as if from a qrencode command and prints your details as contact.vcf.

Underneath it's a real business card. Save contact gives people a proper vCard, and the QR code opens a WhatsApp chat or adds you on WeChat.

What to put on your card

  • Tagline: a word about you, such as Builder
  • Job title: your role, such as AI Engineer · LLM Agents
  • Company: your company, or Independent
  • Email: the one you answer
  • WhatsApp or WeChat: for quick messages

Your card updates as you type, and you can change any of it later.
