Refund Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Payments for Super Namecard are handled by Paddle, our Merchant of Record. Refunds are made by Paddle to the payment method you used.

14-day refund

If you are not happy, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of a payment:

  • Pro: within 14 days of your first payment or of a yearly renewal.
  • Share packs: within 14 days of purchase.

No reason is needed.

What happens after a refund

  • A refunded Pro subscription ends straight away, and your card link stops being live.
  • A refunded share pack is removed from your account. If you had already used it for an export, the next share pack you buy covers it.

Cancelling Pro

You can cancel Pro at any time so it does not renew. Pro stays active until the end of the year you have paid for. Cancelling on its own does not refund the current year; ask for a refund within 14 days of the payment if you want one.

How to ask for a refund

Use the link in the receipt email from Paddle, visit paddle.net to find your order, or write to us at [contact email] with the email you bought with. We aim to reply within 2 working days.

Your legal rights

This policy does not affect any rights you have under the consumer law of the country where you live.
